## Persona walkthroughs (code-based, not real-user tested)

### Persona 1 — first-time smartphone marketplace user
**Task:** open the app for the first time and find someone to fix something at home.

1. **Cold start (non-demo build).** Before any screen explains why, the OS shows the location permission dialog (`locationContext.tsx:143-144`), with text "QuickServe uses your location to find service providers near you…" (`app.json`). *Confusion/abandon risk:* a permission dialog before the user knows what the app is. If they tap "Don't allow", the app silently uses "Benz Circle, Vijayawada" (`locationModel.ts:52-60`).
2. **Login screen** (`login.tsx`): title "QuickServe", tagline "Trusted local pros, booked in 60 seconds.", heading "Log in or sign up", sub "We'll text a 6-digit code to verify your number. No passwords.", field "Mobile number" with "+91" prefix and placeholder "98765 43210", button **"Send OTP"**, benefits "KYC-verified pros", "Itemised quote before any work", "Payments held until the job is done", legal line with "Terms of Service" / "Privacy Policy". *Good:* clear, one field, inline errors ("Enter all 10 digits of your mobile number."). *Risk:* brand is "QuickServe", not "Nearo" (WS1-16). There is no "Browse without login" option (doc 13 §A.1).
3. **Verify** (`verify.tsx`): "Verify your number", "Enter the code sent to +91 …" + "Edit", 6 OTP boxes, **"Verify & continue"**, "Resend code in 0:30" → "Resend code". *Risk:* wrong/expired code shows raw server text (WS1-14). English only.
4. **Role** (`role.tsx`): "Welcome to QuickServe", "Tell us your name and how you'll use the app. You can switch any time from the Me tab.", field "Your name" (placeholder "Priya Sharma"), question "How will you use QuickServe?", cards **"I need a service"** ("Book verified pros near you" · "Transparent itemised quotes" · "Pay only after you approve") and **"I provide services"**, button **"Continue"**. *Confusion:* "Pay only after you approve" conflicts with the visit fee paid upfront in the electrician flow (persona 2 step 6). Name is optional and can't be edited later (WS1-13).
5. **Home** (`(customer)/index.tsx`): location row (caption "Service location"/"Current location", e.g. "Benz Circle, Vijayawada ⌄"), bell, "Hi there"/"Hi {name}", "What do you need help with today?", search 'Search "AC repair", "plumber"…', section **"Choose a category"** with family cards ("Home services" or "Home & vehicle services", "Creators & events", "Beauty & wellness", "Learning & sessions", optional "Sports & venues", "Shop local"), "Ask QuickServe AI" card, banner "Know what happens next", "Top rated near you" + "See all". *Confusion:* two-level drill-down (family → "Choose a service" → category); search only matches category names, so "fan not working" returns "No matching service" (WS1-19). If outside a live city the user sees "Not in {city} yet" with **"Change location"** / **"Notify me when {city} launches"** — clear.
6. **Category → provider → book**: see persona 2. If the first-time user taps a top-rated pro who offers a *package* service (e.g. home cleaning), the profile shows "Visit fee ₹0 / Labour per …" and **"Book {name}"**, which leads to a visit-fee checkout that then fails with "upgrade_required" (WS1-01) — a likely abandonment point.
7. **After booking**: "My activity" tab with segments and "Active / Past"; status pills such as "Payment pending", "Requested", "Quote sent", "Confirm completion". Jargon a first-time user may not understand: "start code", "completion code", "change order", "visit fee", "platform fee", "Seva Coins", "dispute window".

**Financial commitment misunderstandings:** "Pay only after you approve" (role card) and "Payments held until the job is done" (login) vs. the upfront visit fee that is not refunded once the pro is "on the way" (`booking/[id].tsx:446`). Owner: WS2/WS6 for billing truth; WS1 for copy consistency.

### Persona 2 — customer booking an electrician (discovery → booking only)
1. **Home** → types "electrician" in search → "Matching services" shows the Electrician tile (category-name match; E2E `category-electrician` passed) → taps it. Alternatively: "Home services" card → "Choose a service" → Electrician.

Empty: "No electrician pros nearby yet" + "Browse services". *Clear.* Note "Expert"/"Certified" skill labels are self-declared unless verified (not validated here).
3. **Provider profile** (optional tap on card): trust badges ("ID verified", "Bank verified", "Police verified"), stats, "Services & rates" with "Visit fee" and "Labour per job" pills, note "The visit fee is adjusted in the final bill. You approve an itemised quote before any work starts.", footer "Electrician · visit fee ₹X" + **"Book {first}"**. If the pro is in another city the button becomes **"Switch to {city}"** and silently changes the user's location (WS1-26).
4. **Book screen "Book Electrician"** (`book/[providerServiceId].tsx`):
   - "Describe the issue" — chips + text area (placeholder "e.g. Split AC not cooling and making a rattling noise" — AC example on the electrician screen), "Add photo" (gallery only, up to 4).
   - "When" — **"As soon as possible"** ("Pros accept in ~15 min" / "Not available right now") or **"Schedule"** → day chips ("Tomorrow, …") and 2-hour windows ("10 am–12 pm").
   - "Address" — current selection + "Change"; if not a saved address: **"Add house / flat number"** → sheet "Complete your address": "Save as" Home/Work/Other, "House / flat no., street, landmark", "City", "Map pin … Use GPS", **"Save address"**.

Choose a future time.").
   - *Confusion points:* blockers are shown one at a time below a disabled button (user must fix them sequentially); "Platform fee" is listed but "not charged now"; the address must be a *saved* address (`hasAddress = !!location.addressId && !!location.line1`), so a GPS location always forces the extra sheet.
5. **After pay** → booking screen hero "Waiting for {first} to accept" / "Pros respond within 15 minutes. If not, your visit fee is refunded automatically." Cancel item "Cancel booking" with confirm text "Your visit fee will be refunded in full." (hard-coded, WS1-03).

**Could the financial commitment be misunderstood?** The CTA literally says "Pay ₹X & book" — explicit. Ambiguities are about *what happens to the ₹X later* (adjusted, refunded, or paid to the pro) — hand-off to WS2/WS6; WS1-03/WS1-29 cover the copy consistency.
