## Scope & method

**In scope.** Home-service workflows for these archetypes: `inspect_quote` (electrician, plumber, carpenter, painter, AC repair, appliance repair, packers & movers), `fixed` (house, kitchen, bathroom and deep cleaning, pest control, water tank, AC servicing, RO, laundry) and `time_based` (cooks, maid, dishwashing, gardening, babysitting, elderly assistance). Categories come from `supabase/migrations/0056_home_services_catalogue.sql:44-228`. The audit covers one-time bookings end to end, the booking status machine, the inspect → quote → approve flow, and recurring plans (0057) on both the customer and provider sides.

**Method.**
1. I read the **latest** `create or replace` of every lifecycle function. Each one was located with a grep across all 58 migrations:
   - `_set_status` / `archetype_transitions`: 0018, plus rows added in 0019, 0021, 0030 and 0057

   - `accept_booking` and `decline_booking`: 0001

   - `approve_quote`: 0002

   - `_cancellation_outcome`: 0030
   - `prepare_payment` and `record_payment_paid`: 0029
   - `_create_session_booking`, `_cancel_session_booking`, `record_session_payment_paid` and `auto_confirm_completions`: 0030
   - `_finalize_session_completion`: 0043

I read the UI screens: `app/app/book/[providerServiceId].tsx`, `book-fixed/[providerServiceId].tsx`, `src/features/booking/packages/*`, `booking/[id].tsx`, `quote/[bookingId].tsx`, `(provider)/dashboard.tsx`, `recurring/new/[providerServiceId].tsx`, `recurring/[id].tsx`, `recurring/provider/[id].tsx`, `merchant-calendar.tsx`, `merchant-job/[id].tsx`, `src/features/recurring/*` and `src/lib/payments.tsx`.

   - `migration.test.mjs` only applies `0001_init.sql`, `0007` and `0010` (`migration.test.mjs:9,47-48`). Its long inspect_quote lifecycle checks therefore exercise **superseded** definitions.

## Persona walkthroughs (code-based, not real-user tested)

### P1: Customer booking a cleaner or cook 3 days a week
1. **Category → service → "Repeat".** On the provider's book screen, the segmented control "One-time / Repeat" appears only when the master flag, the archetype flag and the category's `recurrence_allowed` are all on (`book/[providerServiceId].tsx` `getBookingModeOptions`). For time-based cook packages, "Repeat plan" is a choice in `PackageBookingScreen`.
2. **"Repeat service" screen.**
   - "How often?" defaults to **Weekly with Mon–Sat preselected** (`:99`). The customer must deselect Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat or tap the days they want. *Abandon/mistake risk: a 6-day plan is the silent default.*
   - "Every how many weeks?" is a raw number field.
   - "When?": Start date (next 14 days only), Start time (hour buttons).
   - "Visit duration (minutes)": free number for an hourly cook with no package. The price changes with it, but the customer only sees that in the quote.
   - "Until?": "No end date" / "End on a date" (types `YYYY-MM-DD`) / "Number of visits".
   - "Address and instructions": the saved address, plus a text area "Add instructions for the provider". *The send button stays disabled until instructions are typed (`:371`). Nothing says they are required.*
   - "Payment" shows "Pay per visit" plus "Weekly, monthly, and prepaid billing will be available after recurring cycle billing is integrated." *(internal jargon)*.
   - Footer card **"Server quote"**: "₹X per visit · 1 visit this cycle", "Platform fee: ₹29", "Total per visit: ₹Y", "Next visits: …". *There is no monthly estimate. "1 visit this cycle" is meaningless for a 3×/week plan.*
3. **"Send request to {provider}".** Toast "Repeat plan request sent." Plan screen shows "Provider response: Pending · Plan acceptance". The customer can already press **"Pause plan"**, which breaks the plan (WS2-16).
4. **After the provider accepts.** Visits appear as "Pending Payment" with no amount or Pay button on the plan screen. The customer must open **each** visit (`booking/[id]`) and tap "Pay session" / "Pay & confirm". *Misunderstood commitment: nothing says each visit must be paid within a window, or that unpaid visits are released 24h before. The only payment-related message arrives after release (WS2-06).*
5. **After paying.** The visit becomes `requested`, and the provider must accept again within 15 minutes or the visit is refunded and dropped (WS2-02). *The customer believes the cook is confirmed.*
6. **Skip, pause, cancel.**
   - "Skip next visit", "Pause plan" and "Cancel plan" each show a confirmation whose message is "charges and refunds follow the existing plan policy". No amounts are shown.
   - On web these buttons do nothing (WS2-07).
   - Cancelling within 12h of a paid cook visit charges it in full and shows it as "Completed" (WS2-14). Cancelling while an unpaid near-term visit exists errors out (WS2-15).
7. **Price change.** If the cook raises the price later, the next generated visits silently cost more (WS2-05).

### P2: Customer needing a plumber who wants to understand the final bill
1. Plumber category → provider → "Book Plumber".
2. "Describe the issue" (required) and up to 4 photos.
3. "When": "As soon as possible" ("Pros accept in ~15 min", an unverified claim) or "Schedule" (7 days, hourly windows).
4. Address card; "Add house / flat number" if no saved address.
5. "Price details": "Visit / inspection fee … Adjusted in your final bill", "Platform fee … Added to the final bill — not charged now", "To pay now ₹visit fee", and "Typical total for Plumber: ₹a–₹b. … nothing more is charged until you approve it." (the range comes from the client `typicalRange`, source unverified).
6. Button "Pay ₹X & book"; footnote "Free cancellation until {name} accepts · full refund if no one does". The server actually refunds in full until `en_route`, so the copy is conservative and correct in direction.
7. After the visit and inspection, the quote card (`QuoteView`) shows items, "Subtotal", "Platform fee", "Minimum visit charge" if the quote is below the visit fee, "Total", "Paid so far (incl. visit fee)" and "To pay now". **This is the clearest bill in WS2.**
8. "Approve & pay" or "Decline quote". The decline dialog clearly says "your visit fee is paid to {name} for the inspection".
9. Confusion points:
   - If the plumber never sends a quote, or the customer never responds, nothing times out (WS2-11).
   - Once "{name} is on the way", cancelling forfeits the fee even if the plumber never arrives (WS2-12/13).
   - "Receipt" in activity is just the booking page (WS2-23).

### P3: Individual electrician or plumber receiving, accepting, completing and getting paid
1. Dashboard "New requests — Accept within 15 minutes or the request expires". The card shows the minimum earning (visit-only settlement), Accept, and Decline with reason chips ("Too far", "Busy right now", "Not my skill", "Other").
2. After accepting, the "Next job" card offers "Start trip", then the exact address and maps.
3. On arrival, "Verify start code": the customer reads out a 4-digit code (5 attempts, then locked).
4. "Create quote": line items labour/part/other, a commission preview ("x% commission applies" on labour; parts "passed through at cost"), and a bill photo for parts above ₹500 (client-only check).
5. "Awaiting approval" or "Awaiting payment" with no timer or exit (WS2-11). The provider cannot cancel in-app at all. A determined provider could call the API and keep the customer's money (WS2-01).
6. "Request completion", then "Verify completion code". If the customer is absent, auto-confirm after 24h.
7. Payout is released 24h after completion (earnings screen copy "Payouts are released 24 hours after the customer confirms completion"). If the customer isn't home at the start there is **no no-show path**. The job sits in `en_route` forever and the provider never receives the visit fee (WS2-12).

### P4: Household cook or cleaning provider managing a recurring plan
1. Dashboard "Repeat requests" card: service, recurrence in words, first dates, then the message **"The pending-plan response does not include per-visit earnings, so no amount is available to display yet."** There is no address area and no instructions. Buttons "Decline" (reason required) and "Accept plan".
2. After accepting, the provider expects the visits to be booked. In reality each visit appears in "New requests" only after the customer pays, and must be accepted within 15 min (WS2-02). Accepting at 11 pm for a payment made at 10:40 pm fails, and the customer is refunded.
3. "Open recurring calendar": a week/day list of recurring visits with "Open visit" (chat-only screen, no actions) and "Plan details". There is no earnings column and no customer name.
4. Plan screen: per visit "Open visit", "Report absence" (destructive; cancels and fully refunds) and "Request reschedule". The reschedule form requires typing `2026-11-25T10:00:00+05:30` (WS2-09).
5. Completing the visit uses the generic booking flow: "Verify session start code" → "Request completion" → "Verify completion code". There is no "customer not home" option and no view of outstanding payments for the plan.
