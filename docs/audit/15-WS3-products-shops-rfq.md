## Scope & method

**Scope.** WS3 covers:
- shop registration and approval;
- the product catalogue: images, specifications, units, indicative price and stock;
- product and shop search, and AI product search;
- shop profiles;
- customer product enquiries and the merchant enquiry inbox;
- WhatsApp and call contact;
- bulk RFQ: request, routing, supplier quotes, comparison, selection and expiry;
- staff access to shop work;
- money flow and commission for products.

**Method.** All findings come from reading code and tests at `bfa999f`. I did not test with real users.
- **SQL.** I read the latest `create or replace` of every function. The main migrations are:
  - `0049_shops_catalogue.sql`: tables, RLS, storage, catalogue, search and enquiry RPCs.
  - `0050_rfq.sql`: RFQ tables and the original `send_supplier_quote` / `compare_rfq_quotes` / `select_supplier_quote` / `expire_rfqs`.
  - `0052_merchant_staff.sql`: overrides `_rfq_*` and `_product_enquiry_participant` helpers, `list_shop_product_enquiries`, `send_enquiry_message`, `mark_enquiry_read`, `get_product_enquiry` and `list_my_supplier_rfqs`. It also adds `get_my_business_shop`, `get_supplier_rfq_for_business` and **`send_supplier_quote_for_business`, which is the quote RPC the app actually calls** (`app/src/features/rfq/api.ts:217`).
  - `0053_activity_contracts.sql`: `list_my_customer_rfqs`.
  - `0051_ai_usage_v3.sql`: AI flags.
  - No later migration (0054–0058) redefines any WS3 function. I grepped for `shop|product|rfq|enquir|quote` function names.
- **App.** I read every WS3 route in `app/app/**` and every WS3 module in `app/src/features/{shops,rfq,ai}`.
- **Tests.**
  - SQL: `supabase/tests/shops_catalogue.test.mjs` (loads only 0049), `rfq.test.mjs` (loads only 0049 and 0050), `merchant_staff.test.mjs` (0052) and `activity_contracts.test.mjs` (0053).
  - Deno: `supabase/functions/ai-assistant/*_test.ts`, run by `scripts/ci/deno-checks.sh`.
  - Jest: `app/src/features/ai/__tests__/productSearch.test.ts`.
  - E2E: `e2e/web-smoke.mjs` and `e2e/all-services.mjs`.
- **CI evidence.**
  - Backend deploy run **44358292571**: the "Deno tests and type checks" and "SQL tests (PGlite)" steps both succeeded (checked with `gh run view 44358292571 --json jobs`).
  - Main E2E run **44365580570** (DEMO project, seeded data). The WS3 steps `phase-7a-shop-and-product-browse`, `phase-7a-product-enquiry`, `phase-7a-rfq-create-and-details`, `phase-7a-owner-submits-shop`, `my-activity-shop-segment` and `phase-7a-admin-approves-submitted-shop` ran and passed. The log's cleanup line `owner_shops_removed:1` confirms the owner-submit and admin-approve steps actually ran; they were not skipped.
  - The failed step `home-family-drilldown-and-marketplace-entry-points` includes the Home → "Shop local" click (`e2e/web-smoke.mjs`). The log does not show which click in that step timed out.
- **Not exercised by any automated test:**
  - the merchant shop editor UI;
  - the merchant RFQ quote UI;
  - comparison UI with real quotes (no supplier quotes are seeded, so the E2E only sees the empty "Supplier comparison" section);
  - product image upload and storage RLS (the SQL tests stub `storage`);
  - AI product search end-to-end (it is turned off in demo mode at `app/app/products.tsx:19`).

**Money for products/RFQ (explicit):**
- **No money flows through the platform for products or RFQs.** There is no checkout, cart, payment or payout.
- `select_supplier_quote` only posts "…no payment was taken." into an enquiry (`0050_rfq.sql:624`). The test `selecting a supplier creates no payment` (`rfq.test.mjs:347`) passes.
- **There is no commission on products or quotes.** Neither 0049 nor 0050 mentions commission or payment. Online product checkout is explicitly deferred (`docs/11-PRD-v2-Implementation-Plan.md:156`, risk R5 at `:575`).
- **The supplier keeps 100% of what the customer pays offline, but no screen says so.**
- **There is no product revenue model.**
